# Prototype 04: audible low drum accents

[Play prototype 04](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_7e25c25aa4d0819189ed2d75282781a0&account_id=personal).

Dylan could barely hear version 03’s additional drums on headphones and good speakers. Version 04 raises only that accent layer by 8 dB, from -33 to -25 dBFS active target RMS. The sound, dark filter, twelve-event pattern, main drums, bass, supporting organ, room and master gain are retained. Accents first occur around 0:07–0:09.

The exact version 03 stems and events are reused; reproduce with tools/research/mix_apocalyptic_prototype_04.py OUTPUT_DIRECTORY. Peak remains -2 dBFS; stereo RMS is -17.48 dBFS, versus -17.51 in version 03. WAV and decoded MP3 remain finite and unclipped. This is still a desktop audition; the installed firmware is unchanged. Listening acceptance of accent level is pending.
