Connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable. Sound runs on the instrument.

Live overview of all four tracks.
FM-1 controls: LEFTHold HOME, tap OCT− for LEFT or OCT+ for RIGHT, then release.

Use the FM-1’s physical buttons and knobs. In Expanded mode, hold HOME and tap OCT− for the left side or OCT+ for the right, then release both buttons. Original uses the firmware's screen renderer. Expanded can show the track overview or an independent live firmware page, with its own track selection. This page requires its matching companion firmware and felucca.wasm from the same build.
