# Taiko bank 05 attribution and license

These four audio assets are adaptations of samples in [SCC Taiko Drums v1.0](https://github.com/floe-audio/Taiko-Drums), by S. Christian Collins, using the Floe-compatible distribution. Most original recordings are by [Subaqueous](https://subaqueousmusic.com/free-taiko-drum-rack/). The library also credits [whatsanickname4u on Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/whatsanickname4u/sounds/95624/) for additional source recordings. Floe supplies the port/FLAC conversion. No image assets are used.

The Floe/SCC distribution states [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). These adapted audio files and the prototype 05 rendered audio are distributed under that license. The original library’s Freesound contribution is separately credited there as CC BY 3.0. This notice makes no claim of endorsement.

Modifications for this T-WRECKS audition: mono downmix, 1.2-second excerpts, tail fades, resampling to 16 kHz and IMA ADPCM encoding. The audition further uses resampling-based pitch changes, velocity scaling, round-robin selection, sequencing, stereo placement, a six-player cap, room processing and peak control. File-specific sources and hashes are in manifest.json.
